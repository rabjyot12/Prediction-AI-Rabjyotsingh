# Milestone 2 Requirements

## Project

**Prediction AI – Startup & Project Risk Analyzer**

## Milestone

**Milestone 2 – Risk Assessment, Scoring, SWOT Analysis and Feasibility**

---

## 1. Objective

The objective of Milestone 2 is to build the risk assessment and project evaluation layer of the Startup & Project Risk Analyzer.

Milestone 2 uses the project information, market information, and competitor information collected during Milestone 1 to identify and quantify potential project risks.

The milestone should provide:

- Risk assessment
- Risk scoring
- Risk classification
- Success probability
- SWOT analysis
- Feasibility analysis

The outputs of this milestone will be used by Milestone 3 to generate strategic recommendations, mitigation strategies, and improvement suggestions.

---

## 2. Inputs

The risk assessment system should use information collected from the previous milestone.

The relevant inputs include:

- Project information
- Market conditions
- Competition
- Team expertise
- Resource availability
- Innovation level
- Market research
- Project budget
- Project objectives
- Market and competitor analysis

---

## 3. Risk Assessment

The system should evaluate the major factors that can affect project success.

The risk assessment should consider factors such as:

- Market and competition risk
- Team expertise risk
- Resource availability risk
- Innovation risk
- Market research risk

Each factor should contribute to the overall project risk score.

---
